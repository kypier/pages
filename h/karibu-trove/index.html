Explore Nairobi like a local

Places worth visiting during your stay.

🏛️
Culture
Karen Blixen Museum
Housed in the former home of the author of 'Out of Africa,' this museum offers insight into colonial Kenya and literary history through period furnishings and photographs. The lush gardens and guided tours (typically KES 1,500–2,000) provide a peaceful half-day escape just 15 minutes south of central Nairobi via Uber.
🌿
Nature
Nairobi National Park
Africa's most accessible safari is just 7 km from downtown, offering game drives where you can spot lions, giraffes, and zebras against the Nairobi skyline backdrop. Entry is around KES 1,500–2,500 per person; hire a guide through your Airbnb host or book online—early morning drives (6–10 AM) are best.
🍽️
Food
The Carnivore Restaurant
This iconic Nairobi institution serves an all-you-can-eat Brazilian-style meat buffet in a lively colonial setting, with prices around KES 3,500–5,000 per person including drinks. It's easily accessible by Uber from most neighbourhoods and offers a quintessential Nairobi dining experience popular with both tourists and locals.
🛍️
Shopping
Westgate Shopping Mall
A large, modern mall in Westlands with international brands, local shops, a food court, and a cinema—perfect for air-conditioned shopping on hot days or rainy afternoons. It's centrally located with secure parking and easily reachable by Uber; expect to spend 2–3 hours browsing or catching a film.
🍸
Nightlife
Brew Bistro & Bar
A popular rooftop bar in the Westlands area serving craft cocktails, craft beers, and casual food in a relaxed atmosphere with views over the city. It's ideal for evening drinks (open from 5 PM), accessible by Uber, and offers a laid-back vibe where you can mix with both expats and locals.
