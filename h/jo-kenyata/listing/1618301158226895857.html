Explore Nairobi like a local

Places worth visiting during your stay.

🏛️
Culture
Nairobi National Museum
This essential museum showcases Kenya's natural history, wildlife, and cultural artifacts in a walkable 2-3 hour visit located in the city center with easy Uber access. Entry is around KES 1,000 (roughly $8 USD), and you'll see everything from dinosaur fossils to contemporary Kenyan art in one comprehensive space.
🏛️
Culture
Karen Blixen Museum
The former home of the author of 'Out of Africa' sits in the leafy Karen neighborhood and offers a glimpse into colonial-era architecture and East African literary history. The museum charges around KES 1,500 ($12 USD) and is best reached by Uber; allow 1.5 hours including the guided tour of the historic house and gardens.
🛍️
Shopping
Westgate Shopping Mall
This modern upscale mall in Westlands has international brands, local boutiques, and reliable parking—ideal for rainy days or finding specific items you forgot. It's easily accessible by Uber and houses a food court with options ranging from KES 500–2,000 ($4–16 USD) per meal.
🌿
Nature
Nairobi National Park
Located just 7 km south of the city center, this reserve lets you spot lions, giraffes, and zebras with the city skyline in the background—a unique urban wildlife experience. Entry is around KES 1,500 ($12 USD), and you'll need a private driver or organized tour (Uber does not operate inside the park).
🍽️
Food
Tatu City Mall
This newer shopping and dining hub in the northern suburbs offers a curated selection of restaurants, cafes, and casual eateries popular with business travelers and families. Expect mains in the KES 1,200–3,000 ($9–24 USD) range, ample parking, and reliable Uber pickup availability.
