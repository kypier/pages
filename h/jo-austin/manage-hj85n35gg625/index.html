Your Domain
Connect a custom domain

Replace pages.kypier.com/h/jo-austin/ with your own domain so guests go directly to yourdomain.com.

How to point your domain here:
  1. Add your domain to Cloudflare (free plan). They will give you two nameserver addresses.
  2. At your domain registrar, replace your current nameservers with the ones Cloudflare gave you. This usually takes a few minutes.
  3. In Cloudflare DNS, add this record:
    TypeAAAA Name@ (root domain) Value100:: ProxyProxied — orange cloud must be ON
  4. Once done, click Validate DNS above to confirm it is set correctly, then save.
