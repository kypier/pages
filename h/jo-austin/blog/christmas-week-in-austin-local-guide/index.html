Christmas Week in Austin: A Local's Guide

By Jo-Austin November 21, 2026 2 min read

Christmas week in Austin is honestly one of my favorite times to be here. The city quiets down just enough that you can actually enjoy it.

Lady Bird Lake Trail: I walk this almost every morning in December. The crowds thin out, the air is cool, and you get the whole downtown skyline to yourself. It feels like a different city.

Franklin Barbecue: I know, I know, everyone says Franklin. But Christmas week the line is shorter than any other time of year. Go on a weekday morning and you might actually get a spot without a two-hour wait.

South Congress Avenue: SoCo does a low-key holiday thing that suits me more than the big 6th Street scene. Good coffee, independent shops, and you can walk from one end to the other in 20 minutes without feeling rushed.

The Local Take

Austinites who stayed in town for the holidays are in a good mood. Restaurants are open, staff are relaxed, and you get real conversation at the bar instead of shouting over a crowd. I always find Christmas week is when locals actually go out, so the energy is good without being chaotic.
