Explore Austin like a local

Places worth visiting during your stay.

🍽️
Food
Franklin Barbecue
Austin's most iconic barbecue spot, famous for brisket that sells out by early afternoon—expect to arrive by 10:30 AM and wait 1–2 hours in line. The brisket sandwich costs around $18 and is consistently rated as the best in Texas, making it a must-do for first-time visitors despite the wait.
🌿
Nature
Lady Bird Lake Trail
A 10-mile scenic loop perfect for jogging, walking, or biking with stunning downtown skyline views and easy parking at the Boardhouse trailhead ($3–$5). It's the most accessible outdoor escape for guests staying anywhere in central Austin, with paddle board rentals and coffee shops nearby.
🏛️
Culture
Texas State Capitol
A stunning pink granite building offering free guided tours daily (9 AM–5 PM, Monday–Friday) showcasing Texas history and architecture—no tickets required. Located downtown with nearby street parking, it's ideal for a quick 1–2 hour cultural visit before grabbing lunch on Congress Avenue.
🛍️
Shopping
South Congress Avenue (SoCo)
Austin's trendiest shopping and dining district lined with vintage shops, galleries, and local boutiques with affordable vintage clothing ($10–$40 per item) and plenty of foot traffic. The area has great Uber/Lyft pickup spots and metered street parking, plus iconic photo ops like the "I Love You So Much" mural.
🍸
Nightlife
6th Street
Austin's main live music and bar district with venues like Hole in the Wall and The Speakeasy featuring live performances nightly, most with no cover charge or $5–$15 entry fees. It's packed Thursday–Saturday and easily accessible via Uber/Lyft, with nearby paid parking garages ($10–$15 for evening).
