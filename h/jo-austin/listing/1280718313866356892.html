Explore Austin like a local

Places worth visiting during your stay.

🍽️
Food
Franklin Barbecue
This legendary barbecue spot serves some of Austin's most sought-after brisket and ribs, with lines forming hours before opening at 11 AM. Expect to spend $15–25 per person; arrive early or use Uber/Lyft as parking is extremely limited on this East Austin street.
🌿
Nature
Lady Bird Lake Trail
A scenic 10-mile hike-and-bike path circling downtown's most beautiful natural feature, perfect for morning runs or sunset walks with skyline views. The trail is free, well-lit in sections, and easily accessible by car or rideshare to any of its multiple parking lots.
🏛️
Culture
The Bob Bullock Texas State History Museum
Located downtown at Congress Avenue, this world-class museum explores Texas history through interactive exhibits and an IMAX theater, with admission around $12–15. It's within walking distance of hotels and has convenient paid parking; plan 2–3 hours for a thorough visit.
🛍️
Shopping
South Congress Avenue (SoCo)
Austin's trendiest shopping and dining district stretches along Congress Avenue with vintage boutiques, local fashion brands, and eclectic restaurants within walking distance. Street parking is available but metered; most shops open by 10 AM and the area is best explored on foot or by bike.
🍸
Nightlife
6th Street
Austin's most famous nightlife strip features live music venues, dance clubs, and bars packed nightly, with free entry to most venues and $3–8 drinks during happy hour (typically 4–7 PM). Rideshare is essential as parking is scarce; the street is safest and most crowded after 9 PM.
